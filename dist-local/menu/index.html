  • Weissbier

    The aroma and flavour of a Weissbier is decidedly fruity and they are very cloudy. These beers are made with at least 50 percent malted wheat, and the hop rates are quite low.

  • Lager

    Lager beers use a process of cool fermentation, followed by maturation in cold storage. It's got a little herbal bitterness and a slight floral aroma from the hops, as well as pleasantly balanced malty cereal flavours.

  • Helles

    A beer for beer lovers. The German-style Helles is a malty pale lager, light bodied and top fermented that balances a pleasant malt sweetness and body with floral noble hops and restrained bitterness.

  • Pilsner

    German Pilsner is a light-bodied and highly attenuated lager beer brewed from 100% barley malt and generally defined as a golden-coloured bottom fermented bitter beer showing excellent head retention and a floral hop aroma.

  • Dunkel (Seasonal)

    This is basically a mild, dark lager beer that is malt forward and gently hopped. It's a very smooth and perfect balanced lager with all the flavours of a dark beer.
